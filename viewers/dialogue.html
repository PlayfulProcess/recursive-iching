Reading as dialogue

The coins fall. Then a few passages tied to what fell are offered, from the text and its layers. You say which ones speak to you. What you choose is the reading.

Read to know yourself, not to be told your fate. Nothing here predicts. The page offers passages from the published text and names where each one comes from; it writes no meaning of its own and never tells you what to do. The cast is three coins in your browser, or your own throw. Nothing leaves this page unless you download it.
