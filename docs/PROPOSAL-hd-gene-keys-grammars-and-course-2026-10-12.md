# Proposal: grammars and a course for the Gene Keys, Human Design and the schools of the I Ching

*12 October 2026, updated the same day (round 2). Companion to [`RESEARCH-gene-keys-and-human-design-2026-10-12.md`](RESEARCH-gene-keys-and-human-design-2026-10-12.md). Items marked "built" exist in this branch as drafts. Nothing here is imported; nothing in recursive.eco was changed. The owner approved the drafts, the 384-line pairing, the origin record, the course and the `ten-wings` addition on 12 Oct 2026.*

## The rule behind every item

Each idea is sorted by what it may contain.

- **Safe now:** the public-domain Zhouyi and Legge's 1882 English; facts that anyone may state (numbers, positions, arithmetic, what a system's own site says it contains); and our own words.
- **Needs permission, or leave out:** any text by Ra Uru Hu (gate names, keynotes, line texts, channel descriptions) and any text by Richard Rudd or Gene Keys (the Shadow, Gift and Siddhi names, line keynotes, book passages, the Integral Human Design keynotes). Jovian Archive and Gene Keys Ltd both reserve their rights; Gene Keys also bars the use of its materials to train or operate AI systems, and this site has an assistant. So: no Gene Keys text anywhere in a grammar.
- **A way to ask,** if ever wanted: Jovian Archive's own Terms and Conditions page says reuse requests must be made in writing by email to office@jovianarchive.com, with the details it lists (its "Contact Us" page is only a web form). Gene Keys: its terms point to a written licence. We recommend not asking. The library does not need either text. We have not contacted either.
- **Autonomy.** Describing structure and stating facts in our own words, with the public-domain Zhouyi and Legge, is within what this library may do on its own. Copying either system's texts is not.
- **Links.** Jovian's Terms ask that its site not be linked without consent. Public pages and grammars cite its pages by title and date only; no live link to jovianarchive.com, the IHDS or myBodyGraph remains in them.

## The ideas

### 1. The 64 gates around the wheel (`hd-mandala-wheel`): **built, draft**

- **Holds:** the 64 hexagrams in Human Design's wheel order. Per gate: the figure and trigrams, the place on the wheel with its zodiac degrees, the opposite gate (the same figure with every line flipped; the Gene Keys call the pair "programming partners") and the centre it belongs to.
- **Sources and licence:** order and degrees are arithmetic, checked against the Fu Xi circle and a practitioner's table; hexagram facts from the repo; the centres are the system's own rule. All CC-BY-SA-4.0 for our words. No Human Design or Gene Keys text.
- **We write:** two short notes (what the wheel is, what is left out). The cards are generated by `scripts/build_hd_wheel.py`, which fails if the list ever stops being the Fu Xi circle.
- **Must not include:** gate names or keynotes, line texts, channels or Incarnation Cross names, any Gene Keys word.
- **Status:** safe now. `_generated: true`, so the importer skips it. If kept, it could join the meta-iching builder as another lens (it has the one thing the existing Human Design grammar lacks: where on the wheel).

### 2. Schools of the I Ching (`schools-of-the-iching`): **built, draft**

- **Holds:** 12 nodes: a guide, the example line (43.3 changing to 58, in Legge's public-domain English and the Chinese), how to read changing lines school by school, then one node per school: classical Confucian (Wang Bi, Cheng Yi, Legge, Wilhelm), images and numbers (Jing Fang, Shao Yong), Zhu Xi, Daoist inner alchemy (Liu Yiming), early Zhou and philology (Shaughnessy, Rutt, Kunst, Redmond), Jung and synchronicity, Human Design, the Gene Keys; and a licence table. Each school node has: who and when, its method, what it stresses, key books with licence status, where a public-domain full text can be read, and an example reading of the same line.
- **Sources and licence:** the Tree of the Changes (which has already checked most of the books), the Cottrell and Yijing Dao pages on Zhu Xi's rules, and the official Jovian and Gene Keys pages. The prose is ours, CC-BY-SA-4.0; only Legge is quoted at length. Marks (✔ ○ ◆ ◇) say how far each claim was checked.
- **We write:** every method summary and every example. The examples say they are illustrations of a school's method, not summaries of what a commentator wrote about this line.
- **Must not include:** passages of in-copyright translations (Baynes, Cleary, Lynn, Harrington, Adler, Rutt, Redmond, Shaughnessy, Smith); anything from the Human Design or Gene Keys texts beyond structure.
- **Status:** safe now. Marked `_source_of_truth: "repo"`, so the importer skips it until PlayfulProcess decides (see "Letting the assistant read it" below).

### 3. The 384 lines on the wheel (`hd-lines-zhouyi`): **built, draft**

- **Idea:** Human Design cuts each gate into six lines of 56'15". The Zhouyi has 384 line statements, six per hexagram. Pair them: a grammar of 384 nodes, one per degree-cell, each carrying the cell's zodiac range and the matching Zhouyi line (Legge, public domain), so a person who knows their Human Design placement (for example "43.3") can read the Zhouyi's own sentence for it without any Human Design text.
- **Sources and licence:** arithmetic from the wheel grammar plus `zhouyi-core`. Safe now. Generated by script from those two, like the wheel.
- **We write:** a one-paragraph note saying that this is a pairing by position, that the Zhouyi's line is not Human Design's line text, and that the two say different things.
- **Must not include:** Human Design line keynotes (the reason this idea exists is to avoid them).
- **Status:** built by `scripts/build_hd_lines.py` (`_generated: true`; 386 nodes: two notes and 384 cells). The Zhouyi text is Legge's 1882 English from `zhouyi-core` (public domain; the Sacred Books of the East scans are cited in the grammar). The pairing by line number is an assumption, stated on the first card. It is the natural "step 3" of `PLAN-meta-iching.md` (the Human Design lens needs content), done without Ra's text.

### 4. The Golden Path map (`golden-path-map`): proposed, structure only

- **Idea:** the Gene Keys' structure as a map: the three sequences, the eleven spheres, which planets set each one, the three leaps in each sequence, the 21 codon rings (as a count and an explanation of why 21: 20 amino acids and one stop signal), the programming partners (computed from the wheel, which this library can do itself).
- **Sources and licence:** the official Gene Keys pages describe the structure openly; we paraphrase it. Safe now for the structure. **Needs permission, or an independent source, for:** which keys belong to which codon ring, and the hexagram-to-codon assignment, because those come from the books. The genetic code itself (which codons make which amino acids) is public fact, but joining it to hexagrams is the system's own convention.
- **Must not include:** the three-band names for any key, the line keynotes, quotes from the books, IHD keynotes. Because the assistant is an AI, no Gene Keys text.
- **Status:** the structure is already in the `school-gene-keys` node. A separate grammar adds little until the codon-ring question has an answer. We suggest waiting.

### 5. Accounts of the origin (`origin-accounts`): **built, draft**

- **Idea:** a small sourced record of how Human Design and the Gene Keys each say they began, in the style of the Tree of the Changes: one node per account, with the date and link, a few words quoted, what the source is and is not, and a ◆ where sources differ (eight days or seven; 3 or 11 January). Nodes: Ra Uru Hu's own words (from the Jovian pages), the school's account (IHDS), the Osho News article, Rudd's account, and an "after" node about when each first spoke publicly. Beside them, a node on what a test would need to look like (blind matching, pre-registration, the Forer effect).
- **Sources and licence:** short attributed quotes (a few words each) and our summaries. Safe now.
- **Must not include:** long quotes of the Jovian blog pages; any verdict on whether the Voice was real.
- **Status:** 10 nodes, hand-written, `_source_of_truth: "repo"`. It includes a node answering whether Richard Rudd was first a Human Design person (yes by his own words and the Gene Keys site; the dating is looser than "first"). Jovian pages are cited by title only.
- **Value:** it makes the question answerable by anyone with the sources in front of them, and it feeds the course's honesty chapter.

## A course: "From oracle to chart": **built, draft** (outline below)

**Why a course.** The existing courses read the 64 hexagrams (Three Lenses) and follow the books (Books of the Changes, Translations). None looks at what happens when a divination text becomes a chart, a calendar and a self-help system. This is the next step.

**Thesis (one sentence, to be confirmed first, per `HOW-TO-WRITE-A-COURSE.md`).** *The same 64 figures have been read as an oracle, a moral guide, a cosmology and a chart of the self; each reading is a map that serves some purposes and not others, and a reading doesn't tell you your fate, it might help you create it.* The honesty spine is the house one: real symbols, a real calendar, real structure; no demonstrated prediction.

**Distinct but rhyming.** It must not open on Ra Uru Hu or Richard Rudd. It opens on the recursive.eco idea (we make belief in order to make meaning; a meaning is at best a map), and the founders earn their place in their own chapters, credited and linked.

## Letting the assistant and the MCP read it

The assistant on this site is the recursive.eco embed. It is grounded on a grammar through `ids.json` (slug to app id) and `_public_now`, and the MCP can read any grammar by id (`get_grammar`, `list_grammars`). **No recursive-eco code needs to change.** The steps, once PlayfulProcess says yes:

1. Remove `_source_of_truth: "repo"` from `grammars/schools-of-the-iching/grammar.json` (keep it to stay out of the app).
2. Import it (the channel import, or `create_grammar` / `sync_grammar_from_json` with the file). It arrives with the visibility she chooses; the repo's convention is Private first, as for `ten-wings`.
3. Put the new id in `ids.json` and, if it is public, add the slug to `_public_now`.
4. Ask the assistant a question such as "how would other schools read this?" on an I Ching page; the embed can then name the grammar. For the MCP, the call is `get_grammar` with the new id, and the node ids (`school-confucian`, `method-changing-lines` and so on) are stable.
5. Optional, for the platform lane (a note was added to `FOR-THE-PLATFORM.md`): a line in the assistant's brief that says "for other schools' readings, consult Schools of the I Ching; never invent a school's reading; label method versus reading". The `corpus_search` tool is an index of another corpus and does not search this repo.
