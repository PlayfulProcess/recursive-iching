The Judgment 卦辭

女壯，勿用取女。

Kâu shows a female who is bold and strong. It will not be good to marry (such) a female.

James Legge, The Yî King (1882), the Text. Words in (parentheses) are Legge's own additions.

Commentary on the Judgment 彖傳 (Tuan, the first Wing)

1. Kau has the significance of unexpectedly coming on. (We see in it) the weak (line) coming unexpectedly on the strong ones.

2. 'It will not be good to marry (such) a female:'—one (so symbolised) should not be long associated with.

3. Heaven and earth meeting together (as here represented), all the variety of natural things become fully displayed.

4. When a strong (line) finds itself in the central and correct position, (good government) will greatly prevail all under the sky.

5. Great indeed is the significance of what has to be done at the time indicated by Kau!

The Image 象

天下有風，姤；后以施命誥四方。

(The trigram representing) wind and that for the sky above it form Kâu. The sovereign, in accordance with this, delivers his charges, and promulgates his announcements throughout the four quarters (of the kingdom).

Legge's Great Image (Appendix II). The Chinese above also carries the Small Images of the six lines.

The six lines 爻辭

Read from the bottom (line 1) to the top (line 6). Each line: the Chinese, Legge's translation, and the Small Image commentary on it.

Line 1 初六

The first line from the bottom, yin (broken, six)

繫于金柅，貞吉，有攸往，見凶，羸豕孚蹢躅。

The first SIX, divided, shows how its subject should be kept (like a carriage) tied and fastened to a metal drag, in which case with firm correctness there will be good fortune. (But) if he move in any direction, evil will appear. He will be (like) a lean pig, which is sure to keep jumping about.

Small Image: 'Tied and fastened to a metal drag:'—(this describes the arrest of) the weak (line) in its advancing course.

If this line changes, it becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.

Line 2 九二

The second line from the bottom, yang (solid, nine)

包有魚，無咎，不利賓。

The second NINE, undivided, shows its subject with a wallet of fish. There will be no error. But it will not be well to let (the subject of the first line) go forward to the guests.

Small Image: 'He has a wallet of fish:'—it is right for him not to allow (the subject of the first line) to get to the guests.

If this line changes, it becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.

Line 3 九三

The third line from the bottom, yang (solid, nine)

臀無膚，其行次且，厲，無大咎。

The third NINE, undivided, shows one from whose buttocks the skin has been stripped so that he walks with difficulty. The position is perilous, but there will be no great error.

Small Image: 'He walks with difficulty:'—but his steps have not yet been drawn (into the course of the first line).

If this line changes, it becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.

Line 4 九四

The fourth line from the bottom, yang (solid, nine)

包無魚，起凶。

The fourth NINE, undivided, shows its subject with his wallet, but no fish in it. This will give rise to evil.

Small Image: 'The evil' indicated by there being 'no fish in the wallet' is owing to (the subject of the line) keeping himself aloof from the people.

If this line changes, it becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle.

Line 5 九五

The fifth line from the bottom, yang (solid, nine)

以杞包瓜，含章，有隕自天。

The fifth NINE, undivided, (shows its subject as) a medlar tree overspreading the gourd (beneath it). If he keep his brilliant qualities concealed, (a good issue) will descend (as) from Heaven.

Small Image: 'The subject of the fifth NINE, (undivided), keeps his brilliant qualities concealed:'—as is indicated by his central and correct position. '(The good issue) descends (as) from Heaven:—'his aim does not neglect the ordinances (of Heaven).

If this line changes, it becomes Hexagram 50, The Caldron.

Line 6 上九

The sixth line from the bottom, yang (solid, nine)

姤其角，吝，無咎。

The sixth NINE, undivided, shows its subject receiving others on his horns. There will be occasion for regret, but there will be no error.

Small Image: 'He receives others on his horns:'—he is exhausted at his greatest height, and there will be cause for regret.

If this line changes, it becomes Hexagram 28, Preponderance of the Great.
